# Tournament operations — prepared, not deployed

Branch: `codex/tournament-operations`, stacked on sessions. Production version: none.

Organizers appoint tournament-specific scorekeepers from active host-club members with accounts. Scorekeepers enter played results only. They cannot organize, schedule, reset, withdraw players or record forfeits. Server authorization is rechecked in the mutation transaction; removing membership/deleting an account clears assignment. Rejoining does not restore it.

For active draws, organizers choose 1–64 tables, estimated match length 5–120 minutes and schedule start. The shared draw engine supplies dependencies for single/double elimination and round robin. Estimates avoid known-player/table overlaps, include a possible reset final and expose a finish time. Called matches remain assigned while rebuilding; entering their result releases table/player occupancy. Estimates are greedy and must be rebuilt after delays. Manual assignments remain available; organizers resolve conflicting calls before generating a new schedule. Unknown players in later rounds are protected by bracket dependencies rather than prediction. Existing manual calls made before this migration cannot be distinguished from scheduled times; call them again after upgrade if still playing.

A free registration place creates a reserved FIFO waitlist offer, not automatic enrollment. Its claim window defaults to 24 hours and can be set from 5 to 1,440 minutes; the registration deadline caps it. Players claim their place or leave the queue; organizers can confirm attendance for them. Reserved places count against capacity. The draw cannot start with open offers unless registration is closed. The five-minute cron removes expired/ineligible offers and offers the next place. The inbox shows positions, offers and claim deadlines; email/push stay limited to result confirmation, actual tournament call-ups and session reminders. Future schedule estimates do not send call-up messages.
